Verse of the Day —

A Peaceful Beginning

When you open Encora Life, you are greeted with a daily Bible verse — the emotional center of your day. No noise. No feeds. Just Scripture.

The verse is designed to feel peaceful, readable, and grounding. A moment of reflection before the world rushes in.

  • ✦ Fresh daily verse, beautifully presented
  • ✦ View full context and surrounding passages
  • ✦ Share intentionally with someone who needs it
  • ✦ Not viral. Not spam. A simple act of encouragement.
How Encora Works
Small Meaningful Actions

Not endless scrolling. Not performance. Not attention.
One meaningful step at a time.

Prayer Requests

Ask for prayer privately. Your need is gently shared with a small number of people — not broadcast publicly. Optionally anonymous. Always safe.

Pray for Others

Receive a request and take an intentional pause. Not a quick reaction. Not a like. A real moment of prayer. Your prayer encourages someone today.

Prayer Circles

Small private groups of up to 12 people — for families, home cells, church groups, and close friends. Private rooms, not public feeds. Just prayer.

Daily Scripture

Every day begins with a verse. Peaceful, readable, grounding. Share it with someone as a simple, intentional act of encouragement.

Stories of Faith

When prayers are answered, share your testimony. No likes, no followers, no ranking. Real stories from real people to strengthen faith.

Global & Personal

An open community of prayer worldwide, combined with small private circles where people know and support one another. Global in mission, personal in experience.
