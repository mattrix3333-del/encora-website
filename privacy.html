Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Encora Life (“Encora Life,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), operated by MattrixCreative, a registered trade name (DBA), collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use the Encora Life mobile application, website, and related services (collectively, the “Services”).

By using the Services, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the Services.

1. Information We Collect

Account and profile information

When you create or use an Encora Life account, we may collect information such as your email address, account identifier, authentication information handled by our authentication provider, preferred language, notification preferences, translation preference, and other settings associated with your account.

Prayer, community, and other content

Encora Life includes community and faith-based features. Depending on how you use them, we may store content you choose to submit, including:

  • Prayer requests and related prayer activity;
  • Content shared within Prayer Circles, including requests, updates, and Circle names;
  • Testimonies and stories of faith;
  • Verse-sharing activity and other encouragement you choose to send or share;
  • Reactions or other interactions with community content.

Please use care when sharing personal or sensitive information in prayer requests, testimonies, circles, or other user-generated content. Information you choose to share with other users may be seen by the people or groups to whom you make it available. Content shown anonymously to other users may still be processed by the service providers described below.

Device, notification, and technical information

If you enable push notifications, we may process a device push token and notification preferences so that we can deliver requested notifications, such as Daily Verse reminders, prayer activity, verse encouragement, or Prayer Circle updates. We may also receive limited technical or diagnostic information necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Services.

Website information

When you visit our website, standard hosting and network systems may automatically process information such as IP address, browser or device type, request timestamps, and similar technical information needed to deliver and secure the site. If we later add analytics, cookies, advertising, or other tracking technologies, we will update this policy and provide any notices or choices required by applicable law.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information we collect to:

  • Create, authenticate, maintain, and secure user accounts;
  • Provide Daily Verse, prayer, Prayer Circle, testimony, verse-sharing, and related features;
  • Display content in the language selected by the user, including translation as described below;
  • Deliver push notifications according to user preferences;
  • Maintain prayer activity, community interactions, and shared content;
  • Respond to support, privacy, safety, or account requests;
  • Moderate content through our own screening and review processes, investigate reports, prevent abuse, and protect users and the Services;
  • Diagnose technical problems, maintain reliability, and improve the Services;
  • Comply with applicable law and enforce our Terms of Use.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information in the following limited circumstances:

  • With other users when you intentionally share a prayer request, testimony, verse, Prayer Circle content, or other community content with them;
  • With service providers that help us operate the Services, such as cloud database, authentication, hosting, translation, and push-notification providers. These providers process information on our behalf or as necessary to provide their services;
  • When reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of Encora Life, our users, or others; investigate fraud or abuse; or enforce our Terms;
  • When required by law, legal process, or a valid governmental request;
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, sale of assets, or similar business transaction, subject to applicable privacy requirements.

Our technical infrastructure includes Supabase for backend/database and authentication services, Expo-related services for mobile push-notification delivery, and OpenAI for language translation as described below. Apple or Google may also process information independently when you obtain or use the app through their platforms. Their handling of information is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

Translation using OpenAI

Encora Life uses OpenAI, a third-party artificial intelligence provider, to translate user-written prayer requests, testimony text, Prayer Circle requests and updates, and Prayer Circle names into other languages. When an eligible reader requests content in a different language, our server may send the written text, the source and target languages, and translation instructions to OpenAI. We do not intentionally include the author's account ID, email address, display name, or device IP address in these translation requests.

The content itself may nevertheless contain personal or sensitive details that the author has written, including health, family, or religious information. Translated results may be stored and reused to avoid repeating the same translation request. If translation is unavailable, content may be displayed in its original language.

Where the Translate My Content setting is available, you can turn it off in Settings to prevent new OpenAI translation requests for content you author. Disabling it does not necessarily remove translations already generated or content already seen by other users. The availability and behavior of this setting depend on the version of the Services you are using. A default-enabled setting should not be understood as a record of affirmative consent where such consent is legally required.

Our existing safety screening and human review processes do not use OpenAI. We use OpenAI for translation, not for safety moderation. OpenAI's processing and retention of data are subject to the terms and settings applicable to our use of its API; we do not promise a specific provider retention period here.

4. Prayer and Sensitive Personal Information

Prayer requests and testimonies can contain deeply personal information, including information about health, family, relationships, beliefs, or difficult life circumstances. Encora Life does not require you to disclose more information than is necessary to use a feature. Please avoid including information about another person unless you have an appropriate basis to share it.

Encora Life is a Christian faith and encouragement service. It is not a medical, mental-health, legal, crisis-response, or emergency service. If you or someone else is in immediate danger or experiencing an emergency, contact the appropriate emergency services or qualified professional in your location.

5. Push Notifications

Push notifications are optional. If you grant notification permission, Encora Life may send notifications related to features you enable. You can change notification categories within the app and can also disable notifications through your device settings. Disabling notifications does not delete your account.

6. Data Retention

We retain account information and user content for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, maintain security and integrity, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Retention periods can vary depending on the type of information and the reason it is processed.

When an account is deleted, we remove associated account information and user-generated content from our active systems through the deletion process described below, subject to applicable exceptions. Some information may remain temporarily in backups until those backups are replaced or deleted under applicable backup practices.

We retain limited internal deletion audit records for security, abuse-prevention, and compliance purposes. These records include an internal account identifier, the type and date of the deletion action, and its result. They do not include the account's email address, display name, or the text of content the user shared. No fixed automatic deletion period is currently established for these audit records.

7. Account and Data Deletion

You can initiate deletion of your Encora Life account in the app by opening Settings (the gear icon on the Home screen) and selecting Delete Account. The process requires confirmation and is intended to permanently remove your account and sign-in access, profile, prayer requests and prayer activity, testimonies and reactions, shared-verse activity, Prayer Circle memberships and the requests and updates you posted, points and titles, and notification settings. Account deletion cannot be undone.

If you own a Prayer Circle with other members, ownership is normally transferred to an eligible active member. Content posted by other members may remain in the Circle. If there is no eligible member to take ownership, your account is locked and the deletion request enters an administrative review process. Completion may be delayed until ownership is resolved or the Circle is authorized for deletion; we do not currently promise a fixed completion time for these exceptional cases.

For privacy questions or assistance relating to your data, you may contact us at encoralife@gmail.com. A support email is not intended to replace the in-app account-deletion process where Apple or another platform requires deletion to be initiated within the app.
